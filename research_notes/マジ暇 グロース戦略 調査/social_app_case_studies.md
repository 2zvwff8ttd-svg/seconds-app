## Q1. Which launch tactics worked (campus-by-campus, ambassadors, waitlists, invite-only, contact upload, TikTok, widget visibility, geo-fencing)?

### Takeaway
The repeatable zero-budget pattern is to launch in one dense, closed community at a time, such as a single school, until a large share of it is on the app within 24 to 48 hours, then move to neighbouring communities. Apps also gained reach for free when the product itself showed up outside the app: widgets on the home screen, link invites that open without installing the app, TikTok tutorials, or the shutdown of a competitor. Paid ambassadors and perks helped BeReal, but at least some of that growth was described as "manufactured". IRL shows where paid or fake growth ends up.

### Cited Findings

**tbh (2017, acquired by Facebook)**
- tbh launched at a single high school in Georgia, chosen because it had the earliest semester start in the U.S. — [Lenny's Podcast notes (podcastnotes.org)](https://podcastnotes.org/lennys-podcast-with-lenny-rachitsky/nikita-bier-how-to-consistently-go-viral-the-playbook-for-winning-at-consumer-apps-lennys-podcast/)
- About 40% of the school downloaded it within 48 hours, and it then spread to neighbouring schools — [podcastnotes.org](https://podcastnotes.org/lennys-podcast-with-lenny-rachitsky/nikita-bier-how-to-consistently-go-viral-the-playbook-for-winning-at-consumer-apps-lennys-podcast/). Other write-ups say "24 hours" ([Antoine Buteau](https://www.antoinebuteau.com/lessons-from-nikita-bier/)) or "first day" ([Intro on X](https://x.com/intro/status/1747373532936634784)). The time window is inconsistent across sources.
- The team set 40% school penetration within 24 hours as the pass/fail test for a launch. They found students through Instagram accounts tagged with the school name and sent them a download link — [Techie + Gamers, Secondary](https://techiegamers.com/nikita-bier-viral-success/)
- Facebook's acquisition price is reported as $30M (Lenny's notes and others) or $100M (Intro on X). $30M is the more widely cited figure — [podcastnotes.org](https://podcastnotes.org/lennys-podcast-with-lenny-rachitsky/nikita-bier-how-to-consistently-go-viral-the-playbook-for-winning-at-consumer-apps-lennys-podcast/); [Intro on X](https://x.com/intro/status/1747373532936634784). See also [Wikipedia: Tbh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbh)

**Gas (Aug 2022, acquired by Discord in Jan 2023)**
- The school-by-school Instagram tactic, as described secondhand: create a private Instagram account named after the school, follow students whose bio names the school, accept all follow requests at once when school lets out, and link the app from the profile. The team repeated this every few weeks, one school at a time — [LinkedIn summary, Secondary](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/raymondchai_nikita-bier-pulled-off-one-crazy-viral-launch-activity-7114171678456233984-FrvO); [Tom Alder LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tom-alder_this-guy-pulled-off-one-of-the-most-exponential-activity-7094647397816930304-Ltoz)
- Gas launched in August 2022 and, by October, overtook TikTok and BeReal as the most downloaded app on the U.S. App Store (WSJ called it the "hottest app") — [Luke Sophinos on X](https://twitter.com/lukesophinos/status/1628767928379379713?lang=en)
- Downloads: 500k+ as of mid-October 2022 — [The Hustle, 2022-10-18](https://thehustle.co/10-18-22-gas-app). 5.1M+ downloads and about 1M active users by early November 2022 — [Wikipedia: Gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_(app)). Discord acquired Gas in January 2023 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_(app)). A claim of "$10M revenue, sold within 4 months" is unverified — [LinkedIn, Secondary](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/raymondchai_nikita-bier-pulled-off-one-crazy-viral-launch-activity-7114171678456233984-FrvO)

**Locket Widget (Jan 2022)**
- Matt Moss first built Locket for his long-distance girlfriend and released it publicly on 1 January 2022. A TikTok tutorial went viral and pushed it to #1 on the U.S. App Store. It had about 2M users within two weeks — [KTLA](https://ktla.com/news/technology/this-22-year-old-created-a-photo-widget-app-to-stay-connected-with-his-girlfriend-and-it-went-viral/)
- Moss's own video got 100k views in two days — [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/life/locket-app-how-to-use-tiktok-photo-widget). His quote: "In about 3 weeks we did about 5 million sign ups" [Company claim] — [whatastartup Substack](https://whatastartup.substack.com/p/he-built-an-app-for-his-girlfriend-and-ended-up-having-80-million-total-downloads)
- Apptopia counted nearly 2M people who had added Locket to their home screen shortly after launch. Fast Company describes the home-screen widget as "a trick hiding in plain sight": every friend's photo appears on the home screen, so the app shows itself constantly — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90712709/locket-the-1-app-in-apples-app-store-uses-a-trick-hiding-in-plain-sight); [iPhone Hacks](https://ijunkie.com/locket-tops-the-app-store-after-going-viral-on-tiktok/)

About 90M downloads and 9M DAU by October 2025 [Company claim via press] — [VnExpress](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/tech/personalities/how-a-gift-for-his-girlfriend-changes-locket-founder-matt-moss-s-life-5106478.html). Another profile says 80M downloads (undated) — [whatastartup](https://whatastartup.substack.com/p/he-built-an-app-for-his-girlfriend-and-ended-up-having-80-million-total-downloads)

**BeReal (2020 launch, 2022 surge)**
- Apptopia attributed the 2022 surge to word of mouth, a college ambassador program and new features. Ambassadors ran campus events with perks such as free boba (Rice University student paper, cited by TechCrunch). TechCrunch also reported that at least some of the growth was "manufactured" to make the app look like a viral hit — [TechCrunch 2022-04-22](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/22/bereal-hype-or-hit-what-to-know-about-the-gen-z-photo-sharing-app-climbing-the-charts/amp)

**Yik Yak (Nov 2013)**
- Launched in November 2013 with geofenced, hyperlocal anonymous feeds. It reached about 200 campuses within roughly 7 months and was used on more than 2,000 campuses by 2016 [Secondary, figures vary] — [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/what-happened-to-yik-yak/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yik_Yak). No good sourcing found on the campus-rep program details.

**Clubhouse (2020 to 2021)**
- Invite-only with a waitlist. Downloads peaked at 9.6M in February 2021 (Sensor Tower; Digital Music News says 9.2M) — [Voicebot.ai](https://voicebot.ai/2021/05/09/clubhouse-arrives-on-android-and-new-features-arrive-in-ios-app-as-downloads-decline/); [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2021/06/22/clubhouse-app-over/). The waitlist and invite system ended in July 2021 (timing is inconsistent across sources) — [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/does-anyone-still-use-clubhouse/)

**Partiful (event invites)**
- Each invite is a lightweight landing page. Guests RSVP through a link with no app or account needed, so invites travel through the texts and DMs people already use. The core product stays free so hosts keep spreading it, and monetization comes from add-ons — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/ca6c792b-7d6d-42a9-ae5f-0970a819c66c/)
- Reported as word of mouth with "zero marketing spend" [Company claim, Secondary] — [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/e7c8f1d8d5a05e35). 500k MAU in Q1 2025 and +400% YoY [unsourced] — [NoGood](https://nogood.io/blog/partiful-marketing-strategy/). Partiful started with the founder's friends, who then hosted their own events [AI-generated encyclopedia; low reliability] — [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/partiful)

**whoo (Japan, Dec 2022)** (see Q3)
- Its growth was driven by the shutdown of a competitor (Zenly). Downloads surged from 3 February 2023, the day Zenly shut down — [whoo/LinQ CEO interview, startup-db (fetch blocked; via search snippet)](https://lp.startup-db.com/media/articles/linq)

**FreeHour (Malta, 2017)**
- Zach Ciappara built it at age 18 as a first-year University of Malta student. It started as a timetable-sharing app, a single utility with a friend-graph hook, and grew into a student super-app (reminders, deadlines, discounts, jobs) — [FreeHour Our Story](https://www.freehour.eu/our-story)
- Its Italy launch reportedly got 20,000 downloads in a day [Company claim] — [Malta Business Weekly](https://maltabusinessweekly.com/crisis-turned-opportunity/17077/); [Gadgets Malta](https://gadgetsmalta.com/general/freehours-road-to-success-maltas-favourite-student-app/)

**Timeleft (dinner with 6 strangers)**
- Started in Lisbon after a failed attempt in Paris. Grew city by city to 185 cities by mid-2024, after launching in the U.S. in April 2024 (35 U.S. cities by August 2024) — [Axios Columbus 2024-08-23](https://www.axios.com/local/columbus/2024/08/23/making-friends-app-columbus-ohio); [Brussels Times](https://www.brusselstimes.com/belgium/1160308/table-for-six-strangers-fighting-urban-loneliness-over-dinner/)
- 320+ cities in 65 countries and "25,000 people every week" [Company claim, 2025 podcast title] — [Audible podcast listing](https://www.audible.ca/fr_CA/podcast/Maxime-Barbier-From-300M-monthly-video-views-to-founding-an-anti-social-media-startup-The-loneliness-epidemic-Connecting-25-000-people-every-week-across-320-cities-in-65-countries/B0FNR7LYM7). The podcast title also says Barbier previously ran a media business with 300M monthly video views, so this is not a zero-audience start. Timeleft's own blog lists 55 cities in 14 countries (a conflicting figure) — [timeleft.com](https://timeleft.com/post/timeleft-the-antidote-to-big-city-loneliness/)

### Inferences
- For a solo developer, the tbh/Gas playbook costs almost nothing: pick one school or one community, find its members through Instagram bios or school tags, open access to everyone at once at a fixed time, and treat about 40% penetration in 24 to 48 hours as the go/no-go signal. Concentrating everyone at the same moment matters more than total reach.
- Widgets (Locket) and link-based invites that need no install (Partiful) both mean that every use of the product advertises it. For a "who's free" app, a home-screen widget showing which friends are free right now, and a web link that lets non-users answer "free now?", are direct analogues.
- TikTok tutorials made by the founder (Locket) worked when the product could be shown in about 10 seconds.
- Paying for perks or ambassadors (BeReal) gets attention, but it can mask weak organic pull.

## Q3. Japan-specific tactics (NauNau, whoo, BeReal Japan, Locket Japan): how did they spread among Japanese students?

### Takeaway
Japan's location-sharing wave was mainly a vacuum created by Zenly's shutdown in February 2023. Apps that closely copied Zenly's interface and were already on the stores at the moment of the shutdown (whoo from December 2022, NauNau from October 2022) inherited a friend graph of junior-high and high-school students almost overnight. BeReal's growth in Japan came later (2023 to 2024) and was concentrated on iOS, which reflects Japanese teens' share of over 90% on iPhone.

### Cited Findings
- Snap shut down Zenly in February 2023 as part of restructuring. Nikkei reported that successor apps such as NauNau rose into the App Store free SNS top 10 — [Nikkei](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUC06ANP0W3A100C2000000/)
- whoo (LinQ Inc.) launched in December 2022. It reached #1 on the App Store and Google Play and passed 10M downloads worldwide within 3 months. Its downloads surged from 3 February 2023, when Zenly shut down — [startup-db LinQ CEO Harada interview (fetch blocked; snippet)](https://lp.startup-db.com/media/articles/linq); [アプリマーケティング研究所 (markelabo)](https://markelabo.com/n/n74d4b2d75ecc?gs=dac59be7d93521e2ad6cf51d432e56f0)
- The whoo CEO said the company nearly went bankrupt from server costs during the surge and chose to prioritize the product over fundraising — [markelabo article title](https://markelabo.com/n/n74d4b2d75ecc?gs=dac59be7d93521e2ad6cf51d432e56f0)
- About 80% of whoo users are junior-high and high-school age (company), who use it for meeting up and everyday contact. Its interface is close to Zenly's, with time-at-location display and a "ghost mode" — [So-net prebell](https://prebell.so-net.ne.jp/tips/pre_24090601.html); [nifty](https://koneta.nifty.com/koneta_detail/1141008023294_1.htm)
- One survey found whoo was the most-used Zenly replacement at the February 2023 shutdown, at 55.5% — [manamina (ValuesCCG)](https://manamina.valuesccg.com/articles/3284)
- MIXI reportedly announced an investment in whoo of up to about ¥2B (snippet only; not verified) — [search result summary; see markelabo](https://markelabo.com/n/n74d4b2d75ecc?gs=dac59be7d93521e2ad6cf51d432e56f0)
- NauNau (Suishow, a subsidiary of Mobile Factory) launched in October 2022, passed 2.7M users in January 2023 and 4.5M downloads (company). BeautyTech describes its Gen Z appeal as a low barrier to entry plus affinity with brands — [BeautyTech.jp](https://beautytech.jp/n/n7f20137c162e?gs=446478eb8b3d)
- A Waseda student interviewed by Daily Shincho (Feb 2023) described the Gen Z mindset of wanting to broadcast home and commute location 24 hours a day — [Daily Shincho](https://www.dailyshincho.jp/article/2023/02131108/?all=1&page=3)
- In October 2023 NauNau exposed the location data and chats of more than 2M users. It suspended service from the 21st and commissioned a third-party investigation — [Nikkei xTECH](https://xtech.nikkei.com/atcl/nxt/news/18/16145/); [KHB](https://www.khb-tv.co.jp/news/15037080)
- BeReal Japan: interest took off from about January 2023 and BeReal became the #1 SNS by downloads in Japan from July 2023 (Sensor Tower). Japanese MAU grew more than tenfold in one year, making Japan BeReal's #2 market by MAU (May 2024). About 90% of usage is on iOS, and the male share is higher than in other markets. It was a regular in the social top 10 on both stores but never #1 on Google Play — [Sensor Tower JP blog](https://sensortower.com/ja/blog/bereal-mau-increased-in-japan); [BCN+R 2024-05-30](https://www.bcnretail.com/news/detail/20240530_428213.html)
- BeReal was positioned in Japan as a "盛らないSNS" (a no-touch-up, unfiltered SNS) because of its 2-minute posting window and no filters. It was also selected as App Store "Today's App" (PR TIMES) — [colorfulbox](https://www.colorfulbox.jp/media/bereal/); [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000013.000133515.html)

### Inferences
- The Japanese pattern: junior-high and high-school friend groups switch apps together, all at once, when a trigger event hits (such as a competitor's shutdown), and spread happens by friend request inside the class group, not through ads. Being installable and familiar at that moment matters more than features.
- Because of iOS dominance (over 90% among BeReal's Japanese users), launching iOS-first, including a widget, is reasonable for a Japanese student app.
- Safety and privacy failures (the NauNau leak) are a major risk in the Japanese market, where parent and media scrutiny of teen apps is high.

## Q4. Why did these apps decline (Gas, tbh, Yik Yak, Clubhouse, BeReal, IRL)? Lessons on retention versus hype

### Takeaway
The declines share three causes. First, single-mechanic novelty runs out (polls, a daily photo, audio rooms). Second, anonymity creates harassment or safety problems that lead schools and parents to ban or reject the app. Third, peak numbers inflated by invite scarcity, paid perks or fake users hide weak organic retention. Rumours or bad press can also stop growth abruptly.

### Cited Findings
- **Gas:** downloads plateaued for about two weeks in late October 2022 after a false rumour (a trafficking hoax) spread — [Wikipedia: Gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_(app)). Discord shut down Gas later [Wikipedia; date not retrieved].
- **BeReal:** DAU fell about 61% from its October 2022 peak by March 2023 (Apptopia) — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2023/02/22/bereal-may-be-on-the-out-users-have-nearly-halved-since-peak/). Dazed's account of its "death" says the novelty faded and Instagram and TikTok cloned the format — [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/61166/1/why-did-bereal-fail-social-media-instagram-authenticity). The company disputed the figures and claimed 25M DAU — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/09/29/bereal-pushes-back-at-report-that-its-losing-steam-says-it-now-has-25m-daily-users). Japan grew while the U.S. declined — [Sensor Tower JP](https://sensortower.com/ja/blog/bereal-mau-increased-in-japan)
- **Clubhouse:** downloads fell from 9.6M (February 2021) to 900k (April 2021). The Android launch gave only a short spike, mainly from India. Cited causes: repetitive conversations, few new features, Twitter Spaces and Facebook copying the format, and weak monetization — [Voicebot.ai](https://voicebot.ai/2021/05/09/clubhouse-arrives-on-android-and-new-features-arrive-in-ios-app-as-downloads-decline/); [Podcast Movement](https://podcastmovement.com/podmov/clubhouse-social-audio-losing-talent-downloads/); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/clubhouse-has-peaked-android-downloads-plummet/)
- **Yik Yak:** harassment and threat scandals led many schools to block it. A 2016 change that required handles and profiles is blamed for a DAU drop of about 75% [Secondary]. The app shut down in April/May 2017 and Square paid about $1M for the team. It was relaunched in August 2021 under new owners with geofencing that blocks high schools, acquired by Sidechat in 2023 and shut down again afterwards [Secondary] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yik_Yak); [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/what-happened-to-yik-yak/); [em360tech](https://em360tech.com/tech-articles/why-did-yik-yak-fail-how-messaging-app-died); [ideaproof](https://ideaproof.io/failure/yik-yak)
- **tbh:** acquired by Facebook in 2017 and shut down by Facebook in 2018 — [Wikipedia: Tbh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbh)
- **IRL:** about 95% of its 20M claimed users were bots. It shut down in June 2023 after a $170M round at a $1.2B valuation — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/26/irl-shut-down-fake-users/)
- **NauNau:** the October 2023 data leak and suspension (see Q3) — [Nikkei xTECH](https://xtech.nikkei.com/atcl/nxt/news/18/16145/)
- **Locket:** Locket kept growing to about 9M DAU by 2025 — [VnExpress](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/tech/personalities/how-a-gift-for-his-girlfriend-changes-locket-founder-matt-moss-s-life-5106478.html). This contrasts with BeReal: the widget gives a passive daily touchpoint that does not depend on a novelty mechanic.

### Inferences
- Positive-only anonymous compliments (tbh, Gas) avoid Yik Yak's toxicity but have a short content half-life. A "who's free" app has a natural recurring trigger (the free time itself), which is closer to Locket and Howbout and suggests better repeat use than poll apps.
- Build safety and moderation in from day one: Yik Yak, NauNau and Gas were hurt by safety incidents and rumours.
- Measure organic D7 and D30 within a cohort (one school) before expanding. Expanding a leaky cohort only speeds up the decline.

### Gaps
- No primary retention cohorts for any of these apps.
- The Gas shutdown date and DAU at shutdown were not retrieved.

## Q5. What do "who's free" / scheduling-social apps (Howbout, FreeHour, Partiful, Bump/Zenly, Timeleft) show about cold start and network effects?

### Takeaway
Planning apps solve cold start by being useful to one person or one organizer before friends join: FreeHour began as a personal timetable, Howbout as a calendar, and Partiful's invites work for guests without an install. They then grow inside dense groups such as a university or a friend circle, where one organizer pulls many people in. Pure "ambient availability" apps (Zenly, Bump) need a whole friend group on the app at once, which in practice only happened through school cohorts or a competitor's shutdown.

### Cited Findings
- **FreeHour:** it started as a timetable-sharing app. Sharing timetables reveals mutual free hours, which is the app's name and core idea. A single university (Malta, a small, dense market) reached over 90% student usage by 2021 (company; another source says about half), and the app later expanded into reminders, discounts and jobs — [FreeHour Our Story](https://www.freehour.eu/our-story); [Malta Business Weekly](https://maltabusinessweekly.com/crisis-turned-opportunity/17077/). It claims 38,500+ daily student users and 95% local youth reach — [freehour.eu](https://www.freehour.eu/)
- **Howbout:** a social calendar built around friend groups and a "chief friend officer" (the organizer). More than 75% of its Gen Z users share their full calendar with at least one friend. It has 4 to 5M downloads and 50M+ events. It raised £500K in UK crowdfunding during the pandemic (2021), then an $8M Series A from Goodwater for U.S. expansion — [UKTN 2021](https://www.uktech.news/news/with-500k-this-uk-based-social-planner-startup-is-transforming-group-meetups-amid-pandemic-20210323); [Business Insider pitch deck](https://www.businessinsider.in/tech/news/a-social-calendar-app-that-helps-friends-sync-their-schedules-and-make-plans-raised-8-million-read-its-pitch-deck-/amp_slidelist/114152320.cms); [startupfundraising teardown](https://startupfundraising.com/library/articles/howbout-pitch-deck-teardown)
- **Partiful:** the host is the acquisition node. Every event sends a no-install web link to N guests, and guests later become hosts. Its Explore surface recommends events that friends are attending — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/ca6c792b-7d6d-42a9-ae5f-0970a819c66c/); [Partiful](https://partiful.com/eventbrite)
- **Zenly to Bump:** Snap acquired Zenly for more than $200M in 2017 and shut it down in February 2023. Zenly co-founder Antoine Martin and 10 ex-Zenly co-founders started Amo and raised $18M at about a $100M valuation. Amo's third app, Bump (December 2023), is described as "Zenly Lite". No download figures are published — [TechCrunch 2023-02-06](https://techcrunch.com/2023/02/06/zenly-co-founder-returns-with-new-social-app-company-amo); [TechCrunch 2023-12-19](https://techcrunch.com/2023/12/19/with-amos-third-app-the-makers-of-zenly-release-a-zenly-like-app); [Maddyness](https://www.maddyness.com/2023/11/20/antoine-martin-le-fondateur-de-zenly-leve-18-millions-de-dollars-pour-son-nouveau-projet/)
- **Timeleft:** this sidesteps the friend-graph cold start by matching strangers. The algorithm forms tables of 6 at a fixed weekly dinner time (Wednesdays), so density is needed only per city and per night. It launches city by city, with local press coverage (Axios Local in each U.S. city in 2024) acting as free distribution — [Axios DC](https://www.axios.com/local/washington-dc/2024/08/18/timeleft-app-dinner-friends-dc); [Axios San Antonio](https://www.axios.com/local/san-antonio/2024/07/18/timeleft-app-lonely-meet-san-antonio); [Connexion France](https://www.connexionfrance.com/news/how-to-meet-new-people-over-dinner-in-france-we-try-out-new-app/682998)
- **IRL** (an event and plans social app) shows that buying users through incentivized installs gives no network effect: its 20M users were 95% fake — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/26/irl-shut-down-fake-users/); [imfounder](https://imfounder.com/entrepreneurship/startup-failures/irl-app-fraud/)

### Inferences
- For a "マジ暇" (free-now) app: (1) give a single user value before any friend joins (for example, their own schedule or free-time view, as with FreeHour's timetable); (2) let a non-user answer an invite through a web link (Partiful); (3) seed one dense cohort (a class, club or school year) at a set moment (tbh/Gas); (4) consider a fixed weekly ritual time ("Friday evening, who's free?") to concentrate activity, as Timeleft does with Wednesday dinners and BeReal with its daily notification; (5) use a home-screen widget for passive visibility (Locket).
- Ambient presence (Zenly-style) has strong network effects but the hardest cold start. Organizer-led planning (Partiful, Howbout) has a weaker effect but works from N=1 organizer.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on Down to Hang, "Plans", When2meet-style social apps or similar small "who's free" apps, nor on their failures. Searches returned nothing usable.
- No Howbout retention or activation numbers. No Partiful guest-to-host conversion rate (none published).
- No Bump download or retention data. The Timeleft launch date is inconsistent (2020 vs 2023) and so are its city counts (55 vs 320).
