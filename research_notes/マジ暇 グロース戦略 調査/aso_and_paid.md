# ASO for the Japanese App Store + Low-Budget Paid Acquisition (Apple Ads, TikTok) — researched 2026-11

Method note: In this cloud session only developer.apple.com could be fetched directly. apptweak.com, adapty.io and ads.apple.com did not resolve, so they would need to be allowed in network settings. Figures from those sites come from search-result summaries, not full-page reads. Treat them as secondary and spot-check before quoting exact numbers.

## 1. Metadata field limits and indexing rules (name, subtitle, keywords, promotional text)

### Takeaway
Apple's official limits are: Name 2–30 characters, Subtitle up to 30, Keywords up to 100 (Apple's reference page says "100 bytes"), Promotional Text 170, Description 4,000. Only name, subtitle, keywords and category are indexed. Promotional text and description are not keyword sources on iOS. Separate keywords with commas and no spaces, and don't repeat words or their plurals across name, subtitle and category.

### Cited Findings
- Name must be at least 2 and at most 30 characters. You can edit it until you submit to App Review, and after that only with a new version. — [Apple: App information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information)
- Subtitle is at most 30 characters and appears under the name on the product page. — [Apple: App information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information)
- Keywords: "Up to 100 bytes". Each keyword must be longer than two characters. Don't repeat the app or company name, and don't use other apps' or companies' names. Keywords are localizable. — [Apple: Platform version information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information)
  - Conflict/quirk: Apple's search page and AppTweak describe the limit as "100 characters". Because the official reference says *bytes*, multi-byte Japanese text could in principle reduce how many characters fit. In practice, App Store Connect is widely reported to count Japanese characters as characters, but I found no source that tested this. **Verify in App Store Connect by pasting a 100-character Japanese string.** — [Apple: Search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/); [AppTweak JA](https://www.apptweak.com/ja/aso-blog/how-to-optimize-your-ios-keyword-field?format=md)
- Promotional Text is at most 170 characters, appears above the description, and can be updated **without a new app submission**. Description is at most 4,000 characters, plain text. What's New is at most 4,000 characters. App previews: up to 3 per localization per device size. — [Apple: Platform version information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information)
- Keyword formatting from Apple's search page: separate with commas and no spaces (e.g. `Property,House,Real Estate`). Spaces separate words inside a phrase. Don't repeat words already in the app name, subtitle, or primary/secondary category. Plurals of words already used count as duplicates. Avoid generic terms ("app", "game"), filler words, and special characters (#, @). **Promotional text does not affect search ranking.** — [Apple: Search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)
- Keyword content that can cause rejection: unauthorized trademarks, celebrity names, irrelevant terms, competing app names, offensive terms. — [Apple: Search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)
- Ranking factors (per Apple): **text relevance** (title, subtitle, keywords, primary category; primary and optional secondary category are both indexed) and **user behavior** (downloads, ratings and reviews, "and more"). Popular terms like "social" bring more traffic but are very competitive for new or small apps. — [Apple: Search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)
- New (2025–26): Apple says "App tags are generated by large language models from your App Store Connect metadata". Promoted IAPs can appear in search results. — [Apple: Search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)
- 2026 practitioner view: title, subtitle and the 100-character keyword field remain the primary iOS index sources, with more weight on relevance than on keyword volume. — [ASOWorld JP 2026](https://asoworld.com/jp/insight/app-store-search-algorithm-2026-what-actually-decides-your-keyword-ranking/)
- AppTweak: repeating a word already in the name doesn't add weight. Separate phrase parts with commas (e.g. `meet,new,people`) so the algorithm can recombine them. — [AppTweak JA checklist](https://www.apptweak.com/ja/aso-blog/apple-app-store-aso-checklist?format=md); [AppTweak JA keyword field](https://www.apptweak.com/ja/aso-blog/how-to-optimize-your-ios-keyword-field?format=md)

### Japanese tokenization quirks (katakana/hiragana/kanji)
- Adapty: Japanese users search with common shortened or colloquial forms, not formal katakana. For example パソコン produces more relevant App Store search suggestions than コンピュータ, so direct translation of English keywords underperforms. — [Adapty: ASO for Japan](https://adapty.io/blog/how-to-optimize-aso-for-japan/) (search summary only)
- **I found no authoritative or tested source** on whether Apple treats hiragana, katakana and kanji variants (e.g. ひま / ヒマ / 暇) as the same token, or how it segments Japanese strings without spaces. See Gaps.

### Inferences
- For a Japanese social app, the 30-character name plus 30-character subtitle (60 indexed characters) carry the most keyword weight. Use the subtitle for the core use-case phrase (e.g. 暇な友達とすぐ会える), not a slogan.
- Japanese words are dense, so 100 characters holds many more concepts than in English. Add script variants (ひま,ヒマ,暇) as separate comma-separated entries when unsure, because the cost is small.
- Each keyword must be longer than two characters (Apple), which may be a problem for one- or two-kanji words like 暇 or 友達. Combining them into longer phrases or putting them in the title/subtitle avoids this. This interpretation needs verification, because it's unclear whether the limit is in bytes or characters.
- Use promotional text for timely messages (campaigns, events), not keywords.

### Gaps
- No primary or tested source on Japanese segmentation, or on whether kana and kanji variants are equivalent in the iOS index.
- Unclear whether the keyword limit is counted in bytes or characters for Japanese, and whether the ">2 characters per keyword" rule applies to Japanese characters.

## 2. Which additional localization is indexed for the Japan storefront?

### Takeaway
Apple's App Store localizations table lists the Japan (JPN) storefront with default language **Japanese** and additional language **English (US)**. So both your ja and en-US metadata (name, subtitle, keywords) count for Japan, which roughly doubles the keyword space.

### Cited Findings
- Exact row from Apple's table: `| JPN | Japan | Japanese | English (US) |`. The language a customer sees depends on their device language, the storefront language, the languages you added, and your primary language. — [Apple: App Store localizations](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-store-localizations/)
- AppTweak has a dedicated guide on cross-localization (adding indexed keywords through a secondary localization). — [AppTweak: cross localization](https://www.apptweak.com/ja/aso-blog/how-to-benefit-from-cross-localization-on-the-app-store?format=md) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- Fill English (U.S.) metadata with additional **Japanese or romaji** keywords that don't fit in the ja localization, not just an English translation. This is a common ASO tactic: the en-US name, subtitle and keyword field add roughly another 160 indexed characters for Japan.
- Caveat: en-US metadata is also what US storefront users see. If the app isn't launching in the US, that cost is low.

### Gaps
- Apple's table doesn't explicitly say "indexed for search". The claim that the additional language's keywords are indexed comes from the table's purpose plus practitioner consensus (AppTweak cross-localization). Whether en-US keywords carry the same weight as ja keywords is unverified.

## 3. Ratings and reviews: requestReview rules and evidence

### Takeaway
The system prompt appears at most **3 times per app per 365 days**. Calling the API doesn't guarantee it shows, and you get no callback. Apple says to ask after demonstrated engagement or a completed task, never at first launch or during onboarding, and to wait at least 1–2 weeks between requests. The evidence that prompts raise rating counts is moderate: Apptentive measured a +15% median rating count after SKStoreReviewController launched (2017, older data).

### Cited Findings
- HIG (quoted): "The system automatically limits the display of the prompt to three occurrences per app within a 365-day period." — [Apple HIG: Ratings and reviews](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/ratings-and-reviews)
- HIG: "Ask for a rating only after people have demonstrated engagement… for example… when they complete a game level or a significant task." Look for natural breaks. Possible signals are launch count or frequency, features explored, and tasks completed. — [Apple HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/ratings-and-reviews)
- HIG: "Avoid asking for a rating on first launch or during onboarding… People may even be more likely to leave negative feedback." "Consider allowing at least a week or two between requests." Don't interrupt a task. — [Apple HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/ratings-and-reviews)
- HIG: When you release a new version, you can reset the summary rating. Resetting means fewer ratings overall, which can discourage downloads, so weigh the trade-off. — [Apple HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/ratings-and-reviews)
- APIs: SwiftUI `@Environment(\.requestReview)` (RequestReviewAction). UIKit/StoreKit `AppStore.requestReview(in: windowScene)`. The old `SKStoreReviewController.requestReview()` without a scene is deprecated. (From model knowledge; Apple's StoreKit doc page didn't render in the fetch.) — [Apple: Requesting App Store reviews](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/requesting-app-store-reviews)
- Evidence: Apptentive reported the median number of app ratings rose **15%** after Apple introduced SKStoreReviewController. This is a cross-app median, not a controlled study, and dates from **2017 (old)**. — [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/study-apple-update-increases-app-ratings-by-15/445029/)
- No callback means you can't know whether the prompt showed or whether the user rated. — [avanderlee](https://www.avanderlee.com/swift/skstorereviewcontroller-app-ratings/)
- Apple developer forums (2025) have a thread reporting a significant drop in ratings tied to requestReview behavior (anecdotal). — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/794961)
- Apple also offers "Ratings, reviews, and responses" guidance. Developer responses to reviews are managed in App Store Connect. — [Apple: Responding to reviews](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/responding-to-reviews/)
- Ratings and reviews are an explicit user-behavior ranking factor. — [Apple: Search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)

### Inferences
- For a social or meetup app, good prompt moments are right after a successful match or meetup, or after the Nth positive interaction (e.g. the 3rd accepted invite). Never prompt after a declined or ignored invite.
- With only 3 shows per year, gate requests with your own logic (e.g. at least 2 successful meetups, at least 7 days since install, at least 14 days since the last request).

### Gaps
- No recent (2024–26) controlled study of prompt timing versus rating volume. No Japan-specific data.

## 4. In-App Events (IAE)

### Takeaway
Up to **10 published** and **15 approved** events at a time. Each event lasts up to **31 days** and can be promoted up to **14 days before** it starts. Text limits: name 30, short description 50, long description 120. Events are submitted independently of app versions. In search, users who already have the app see the event card; non-users see screenshots. People can also find events by searching for the event directly.

### Cited Findings
- Limits: up to 15 approved events in App Store Connect and up to 10 published per app at a time. Maximum duration 31 days. Promotion up to 14 days before start. Dates can vary by region. Events can be submitted without a new app version. — [Apple: In-App Events](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events/)
- Metadata: event name ≤30 characters, short description ≤50, long description ≤120. — [Apple: In-App Events](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events/)
- Badges: Challenge, Competition, Live Event, Major Update, New Season, Premiere, Special Event. — [Apple: In-App Events](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events/)
- Search: users with the app see the event card next to your app. Users without it see screenshots. Searching for the event itself shows the card with your app. Events can be featured on Today, Games and Apps tabs. Sorted by start time, with high priority first. — [Apple: In-App Events](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events/); [Apple: Search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)
- Purpose options for personalization: attract new users, keep active users informed, bring back lapsed users, or all users. — [Apple: In-App Events](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events/)
- Rejection risks: specific prices, "best/#1" claims, all caps, call-to-action event names (e.g. "Watch the livestream"), and using the description to promote the app. Media: card 16:9 (min 1920×1080), detail 9:16 (min 1080×1920). — [Apple: In-App Events](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events/)

### Inferences (social-app examples)
- Possible event ideas: 「金曜夜の暇つぶし会」 (weekly Live Event or Special Event), 「夏休みヒマ友チャレンジ」 (Challenge), seasonal moments (花火大会, 年末年始, GW), and Major Update for new features.
- Event names are indexed in search, so they add searchable, keyword-bearing text beyond the 100-character field. This follows from "search for events directly" and is not a guarantee of keyword ranking.
- Keep 2–4 rolling events so there is always something live, which also feeds the re-engagement purpose.

### Gaps
- No quantitative Japan data on IAE lift for social apps.

## 5. Custom Product Pages (CPP) and Product Page Optimization (PPO)

### Takeaway
Up to **70 CPPs** (each varying screenshots, previews and promotional text). CPPs can be assigned keywords for organic search, can deep link (iOS 18+), and can be used in Apple Ads. Apple says CPPs average a +2.5 percentage point conversion lift (to about 4.1% vs. a 1.6% default-page average). PPO allows up to **3 treatments** against the original, runs up to **90 days**, and Apple recommends at least 90% confidence before acting.

### Cited Findings
- CPP: up to 70 additional versions. Vary screenshots, promo text and previews. You can assign keywords so a CPP shows in search instead of the default page, and each keyword combination maps to only one page. Deep links supported on iOS/iPadOS 18+. Can be used in Apple Ads (Today tab, Search tab, search results). Reviewed independently of app updates. Analytics include impressions, downloads, conversion and retention. — [Apple: Custom product pages](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/)
- Apple's claim: developers see "an average increase of 2.5 percentage points" when referring people to a CPP, "a 156% increase over the 1.6% average conversion rate on default pages". — [Apple: Custom product pages](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/)
- PPO: up to 3 treatments, up to 90 days, and you set the traffic share. Example: 40% allocation with 2 treatments gives 20% each. Apple recommends at least 90% confidence. One test at a time, and a test can't be edited after it starts. Test icon, screenshots and previews. Alternate icons must be in the binary. Tests without icons can be submitted without a new version. Shown on iOS 15+. — [Apple: Product Page Optimization](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/)

### Inferences
- With low traffic, PPO takes a long time to reach 90% confidence. Test big differences first (e.g. the first screenshot's message) with at most 1–2 treatments.
- Create CPPs per audience or channel (e.g. a TikTok-traffic page, a university-student page) and link ad URLs to them. Assign CPP keywords for intent clusters (e.g. 暇つぶし vs 友達作り).

### Gaps
- No Japan-specific CPP/PPO lift data.

## 6. Screenshots and conversion benchmarks

### Takeaway
Up to 3 screenshots or previews can appear in search results, so the first 1–3 frames decide the tap. Benchmarks: Apple's own default-page conversion average is 1.6% (CPP pages about 4.1%). AppTweak (US, 2025) reports 3.8% average install rate from search/browse impressions and 8.56% page-view-to-download. Japan all-category data from 2019 shows about 2.6% impression-to-install.

### Cited Findings
- Search results can show the rating and up to 3 screenshots or app previews, depending on platform and orientation. — [Apple: Search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)
- PPO assets (icon, screenshots) can appear in search and on Today, Games and Apps tabs. — [Apple: PPO](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/)
- Default product page average conversion 1.6%; CPP adds +2.5 pp. — [Apple: CPP](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/)
- AppTweak 2025 (US, all categories): 3.8% average install rate from search/browse impressions and 8.56% average page-view-to-download. — [AppTweak: Average conversion rate per category 2025](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/average-app-conversion-rate-per-category?currency=us) (via search summary)
- Mobile Action (2019, Japan, about 2,500 apps): roughly 119 product page views and 26 installs per 1,000 search impressions, about 2.6% impression-to-install. **Old (2019).** — [Mobile Action: Japan benchmarks](https://mobileaction.co/blog/app-store-search-download-benchmarks-japan)
- Adapty lists a 13.1% conversion rate for the Social Networking category, but the market, year and metric definition aren't stated, so don't rely on it. — [Adapty: App Store conversion rate](https://adapty.io/blog/markdown/app-store-conversion-rate.md)

### Inferences
- Reasonable planning assumptions for a new Japanese social app: about 2–4% impression-to-install and about 5–10% page-view-to-install. Measure your own in App Analytics.
- Screenshot practice for Japan: dense, large Japanese caption text with a clear benefit on frame 1. Portrait 6.9" screenshots. Show real UI with people or friends in context. (Practitioner consensus; I found no 2025 Japan-specific study.)

### Gaps
- No 2025–26 Japan-specific Social Networking conversion benchmark found. AppTweak's country/category tool would provide one but wasn't accessible.

## 7. Apple Ads (formerly Apple Search Ads) in Japan

### Takeaway
Japan is a relatively cheap Apple Ads market. AppTweak's 2025 median search-results CPT for Japan is about **$1.11** (US $1.91). The US Social Networking CPI is about $1.45 and its median CPT about $0.90. **Basic** uses CPI pricing with a cap of **$10,000/month per app**. **Advanced** uses CPT pricing with daily campaign budgets and no budget maximum. I found no Apple-stated minimum spend.

### Cited Findings
- Japan median search-results CPT is $1.11 vs. $1.91 in the US (AppTweak 2025, all categories). Global median CPT is about $0.92 and global median CPI about $1.80. Social Networking CPI is $1.45 (one of the cheapest categories). US Social Networking median CPT in 2026 is $0.90. — [AppTweak: Apple Ads benchmarks](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-ads-benchmarks) (via search summary; the page mixes 2025 and 2026 labels)
- Mobile Action lists Japan among the lowest-CPT regions (2024 data). — [Mobile Action: Region CPT](https://www.mobileaction.co/report/apple-search-ads-2025-benchmark-report/region-cost-per-tap/)
- Basic: maximum monthly budget **$10,000 (US) per app per month**, one budget across all selected countries. You set a max CPI, and Apple maximizes installs up to that CPI and budget. — [Apple Ads: Compare solutions](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/apple-ads-basic/0001-compare-apple-ads-solutions); [Apple Ads Basic](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/basic) (via search summary)
- Advanced: cost per tap (you pay only on tap, up to your max CPT bid), daily budget at campaign level, no budget maximum. Placements include Today tab, Search tab, search results and product pages. — [Apple Ads: How pricing works](https://searchads.apple.com/help/advanced/0003-how-pricing-works)
- "No minimum spend" is claimed by a third-party guide, not confirmed on an Apple page. — [Adapty: Apple Search Ads 2026](https://adapty.io/blog/apple-search-ads/)
- Apple Ads can send traffic to a CPP and deep link. — [Apple: CPP](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/)

### Inferences
- At about ¥150/$ (unverified rate), Japan CPT of about $1.11 is roughly ¥165 per tap. With 40–60% tap-to-install, CPI would be roughly ¥280–420. This is a rough estimate. Exact-match brand and niche keywords (e.g. 暇, 遊び相手) are usually much cheaper than generic ones like 友達.
- For about ¥30–50k/month: use Advanced with exact-match keywords, a Search Match discovery group with a low bid, and CPP links. Basic is simpler but gives no keyword control.

### Gaps
- No Japan × Social Networking CPT or CPI figure found. AppTweak says Japan has relatively high CPI but gives no number, which conflicts somewhat with its low CPT.

## 8. TikTok ads in Japan (CPM/CPI, minimums, Spark Ads, Promote)

### Takeaway
In Ads Manager, the minimum daily budget is about **$50 per campaign and $20 per ad group** (official help, USD). Third-party 2026 estimates put Japan CPM at about **$5.20–9.80**. Spark Ads (boosting organic posts) bill on the same CPM basis and tend to have slightly cheaper CPMs. The in-app **Promote** feature can start from about **¥500/day**, according to a blog. I found no Japan-specific CPI benchmark of good quality.

### Cited Findings
- Official minimums (Ads Manager): daily budget at least $50 at campaign level and at least $20 at ad group level. Minimums vary by optimization goal. TikTok recommends a $20/day minimum ad group budget for APAC web conversions. — [TikTok Ads help: Budget (JA)](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget?lang=ja); [TikTok: Budget best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget-best-practices?lang=en) (via search summary)
- Japan average TikTok CPM is about $5.20–9.80 (third-party 2026). — [Stackmatix: TikTok ads cost by country 2026](https://stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-by-country-2026)
- General in-feed CPM is $3–12. App promotion CPI is about $0.50–1.80 (global, third-party, 2026). — [Business of Apps](https://businessofapps.com/marketplace/tiktok/research/tiktok-ads-cost); [Zapier](https://www.zapier.com/blog/tiktok-ads-cost)
- Spark Ads use CPM like in-feed, at about $4–8. AdLiftr 2026 data shows Spark CPM $6.20 vs. standard $7.40 (1.19x cheaper). — [WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/tiktok-ads-cost); [Metricool 2026](https://metricool.com/tiktok-ads-cost/)
- Japanese agency: auction ads can start from ¥5,000/day. Reserved in-feed placements start around ¥420,000. — [stock-sun 2026](https://stock-sun.com/column/tiktokads-cost/)
- Japanese articles recommend amplifying videos that already perform well organically (Spark-style). — [Ownly SSLab](https://www.ownly.jp/sslab/tiktok-advertising)
- Promote: minimum about ¥500/day (blog, not official). The claim of 1.7–2.3x more views than normal posts is unsourced. Users must be 18+ (official). — [assist-all blog](https://lifestyle.assist-all.co.jp/tiktok-promote-overview-cost-effectiveness/); [TikTok help: About Promote (JA)](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-promote-on-tiktok?lang=ja)

### Inferences
- A minimum Ads Manager test runs about $50/day × 7 days, roughly ¥50k+ per week. Promote (from ¥500/day) is the cheapest way to test which organic videos convert before moving them to Spark Ads.
- At $5–10 CPM, ¥10k buys roughly 7–13k impressions. App-install CPI in Japan is likely around ¥200–600 for broad social targeting, but this is extrapolated from global ranges and needs real testing.
- Note that TikTok's audience skews young. Many 18–24 users are reachable, but under-18 ad targeting is restricted.

### Gaps
- No official Japan CPI benchmark. The yen minimum in Ads Manager for Japanese-currency accounts wasn't confirmed (the JA help table couldn't be read). Promote pricing per view or follower isn't public.
